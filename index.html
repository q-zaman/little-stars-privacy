Privacy Policy: Little Stars

Last updated: 2026-11-12

Developer: Meclay Games

Contact: support@meclay.app

In short

  • Little Stars is a learning app for young children (about ages 2 to 6).
  • We do not collect personal information from children or from parents. There are no accounts, no analytics, no tracking SDKs of our own, no social features and no in-app purchases.
  • The app shows a small banner ad from Google AdMob on the Home screen only. Ads are set up for children: not personalized, no advertising ID, and only content rated for all ages. There are no ads in the lessons, the games or the parent area.
  • Everything the app remembers (settings and progress) stays on your phone.

Information we collect

None. We do not collect, receive or store on any server: names, email addresses, phone numbers, addresses, location, photos, contacts, microphone or camera data, advertising identifiers, usage analytics or crash reports. The app does not ask for any of these permissions.

Information kept only on your phone

So that the parent area works, the app saves a few things only on your device:

  • Parent settings: sound on/off, voice speed, play-time limit, quiet hours, which lessons and games are switched on, chosen region and languages, the child's age group and an optional first name.
  • The parent PIN, stored as a scrambled (salted hash) value. The PIN itself is not stored.
  • Progress: play time per day, stars, which letters, numbers and animals were seen, and which answers were right or wrong.

This information never leaves your phone and is never shared. A parent can erase the progress at any time (Parent area, then Reset progress). Uninstalling the app deletes everything.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

Little Stars shows one small banner ad, labelled "Ad", in a strip at the bottom of the Home screen. Ads are provided by Google AdMob (Google LLC). We have set the app up for children as Google requires:

  • Child-directed treatment is always on. Ads are requested as for a child audience, and under the age of consent.
  • No personalized ads. Ads are not based on a child's interests, behavior or history, and there is no remarketing or profiling.
  • No advertising ID. The app does not use or send the Android advertising ID.
  • Only ads rated for all ages (the lowest content rating, "G") are requested.
  • Placement: only the Home screen. Never in a lesson, a game, the parent area or the break screen, and never over a button.

To show an ad, the AdMob software on the phone contacts Google over the internet. Google receives technical information that any ad request contains, such as the device's IP address, device model, operating system version, language, the app's name and version, and the ad request itself. For child-directed apps Google limits how it may use this information. We do not receive or see this information. Google explains its handling in the Google Privacy Policy (policies.google.com/privacy) and in "How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services" (policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

Tapping an ad opens a web page or a store page outside the app. Please keep an eye on young children, or use your phone's parental controls.

Internet permission

Android lists the "internet" permission for this app for two reasons: (1) to load the Google AdMob banner described above, and (2) the 3D animal viewer, which shows a small web page that the app serves to itself on your phone (address 127.0.0.1). Apart from the ad request, the lessons and games send and receive no data over the internet. Plain-text network use is blocked everywhere except to the phone itself.

Children

Little Stars is designed for children. The child area has no purchases, no links to other websites or apps (other than a tapped ad, see above) and no way to contact anyone. The parent area is protected by a 4-digit PIN. We design the app to follow the Google Play Families Policy, the AdMob program policies for child-directed apps, and the principles of children's privacy laws such as COPPA (USA) and the GDPR for children (Europe). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Third-party services

  • Google AdMob (Google LLC): shows the banner ad, as described above.
  • The app also uses open-source software libraries that run on the device and do not collect data.

We do not use any other advertising or analytics service.

Data security and retention

We hold no data about you or your child. Data kept on the phone is protected by the phone's own security. The PIN is stored as a salted hash. Backups of app data to the cloud are switched off for this app.

Your choices and rights

You can erase all saved progress in the Parent area or by uninstalling the app. Because we hold no personal data, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete on our side. For questions about this policy or your child's privacy, contact us using the address at the top. For Google's handling of ad requests, contact Google.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be shown in the app (Parent area) and on the web address given in the store listing, and the date above will be updated.
